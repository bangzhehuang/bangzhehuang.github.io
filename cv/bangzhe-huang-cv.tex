\project
  {Scaling Foundation Models for Many-Body Physics}
  {Project lead; advisor: Prof. Jing Wang, Department of Physics, Fudan University; preprint in preparation}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Built and trained a \textbf{Hamiltonian-conditioned foundation model}
  for condensed-matter physics spanning transverse-field Ising, Heisenberg,
  XXZ/XYZ, and Dzyaloshinskii--Moriya interactions.
  \item Led the end-to-end foundation-model engineering stack: physical-system
  representation, multi-task data generation, multi-GPU JAX training,
  mixed precision, resumable checkpoints, and real-time experiment monitoring.
  \item Integrated mixture-of-architectures feature fusion with neural
  quantum-state learning and physics-based evaluation to model ground-state
  wavefunctions across thousands of Hamiltonians.
\end{itemize}

\project
  {Open-Ended Scientific Research Benchmark for Physics Research Agents}
  {Independent research; academic discussion: Prof. Siheng Chen, School of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai Jiao Tong University; pilot completed}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Designed open-ended physics research tasks with stable ground truth
  defined by low-dimensional physical structures and equivalent observables,
  without prescribing a single valid research trajectory.
  \item Built an end-to-end pilot spanning experiment and simulation design,
  code generation and execution, multi-round observation and feedback, and a
  final physical audit.
\end{itemize}

\project
  {Phase Transport and Defect Dynamics in Nonreciprocal Spin Systems}
  {Lead researcher; advisor: Prof. Zi Cai, School of Physics and Astronomy, Shanghai Jiao Tong University; pilot completed}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Used Monte Carlo simulations to study the microscopic origin of
  macroscopic oscillations in two-dimensional nonreciprocal spin systems and
  extracted oscillation frequency, domain-wall density, and defect observables.
  \item Connected oscillation frequency $\omega$, domain-wall density $\rho$,
  and effective transport velocity through $\omega=\rho v_{\mathrm{eff}}$, and
  extracted $v_{\mathrm{eff}}$ from the linear $\omega$--$\rho$ regime.
  \item Identified regimes dominated by vortex-pair generation and periodic
  wall expansion versus vortex-constrained topological structures, and tested
  the physical picture across lattice geometries and symmetric bond disorder.
\end{itemize}
